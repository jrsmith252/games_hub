Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version

Game Hub does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, Game Hub saves small pieces of data in your device's local storage: puzzle progress, best scores, win/loss counts, and whether you have seen a game's instructions. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data or your browser's site data deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking or social SDKs.
  • Fonts and all game assets are bundled with the app, so playing makes no third-party network requests.

Children

Game Hub contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no data collection, so it is suitable for all ages.
