How to play

  1. Drag a block to slide it around the board. Blocks can't pass through each other or the grey stones.
  2. Each block leaves through a door of its own colour in the wall. Drag it into the door and out it goes, as long as the whole block fits through the opening.
  3. Blocks with arrows only move the way the arrows point.
  4. Clear every block. The clock counts up while you play: beat the target time for 3 stars, or finish within half as long again for 2. You can't run out of time.
  5. The clock pauses whenever this help, the level list or another app is open.

Stuck? Hint shows a good next move. You get 2 hints per level, and each one costs a star. Undo and Restart are free.

All clear!
