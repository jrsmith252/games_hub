How to play

  1. Drag a block to slide it through the empty squares. Blocks can't pass through each other, and the board is packed, so you'll need to shuffle blocks through the gaps to make room.
  2. Each coloured block leaves through the door of its colour in the wall, as long as the whole block fits through the opening. Slide it up to the door, or just let go (or tap it) when it has a clear straight run, and out it goes.
  3. Blocks with arrows only slide the way the arrows point.
  4. Some levels have grey crates: they never leave, so push them out of the way.
  5. Get every coloured block out before the clock runs out. Finish with half the time left for 3 stars, a quarter for 2. Out of time? Try again: it's free. The clock pauses while this help, the level list or another app is open.

Undo is free but the clock keeps running. Restart starts the level, and the clock, again.
