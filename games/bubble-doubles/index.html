How to play

4+4→8
  1. Drag to aim and let go to shoot. Bubbles bounce off the side walls.
  2. When your bubble touches the same number, they merge and double. Three 4s make a 16: each extra bubble doubles it again.
  3. The new number can merge again with its neighbours, so set up chains.
  4. Bubbles cut off from the top drop away for extra points. Make 2048 and it bursts, clearing everything around it.
  5. Every few shots a new row pushes down. If the bubbles reach the dashed line, the game ends.
  6. Swap trades the bubble you're about to shoot for the next one.
