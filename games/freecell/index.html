FreeCell
Shuffling a winnable deal…

How to play

  1. Build the four piles at the top right from Ace up to King, one suit each.
  2. Every card is face up. In the columns, stack cards downwards in alternating colours (a red 6 on a black 7).
  3. The four free cells at the top left each hold one card while you dig out the one you need.
  4. Any card can go into an empty column. You can move a run of cards at once if you have enough free cells and empty columns to shuffle them through.
  5. Drag cards where you want them, or just tap a card and it goes to the best place it can.
