How to play

  1. Put every domino on the board so that it's covered.
  2. Each coloured region has a rule in its corner, and the spots on its squares must follow it:
  1. Squares with no colour have no rule.
  2. Drag a domino onto the board. Or tap a domino, then tap a square.
  3. Tap a domino to turn it, in the tray or on the board. Drag it off the board to take it back.
  4. A rule's diamond turns green when it's met and red if it can't be.
