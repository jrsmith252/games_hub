How to play

  1. Move along the top to choose where to drop, and let go to drop the bubble into the jar.
  2. When two bubbles with the same number touch, they fizz together into the next, bigger bubble: 2 + 2 makes 4, 4 + 4 makes 8, all the way up to 2048.
  3. Two 2048s together burst for a big bonus.
  4. Bigger bubbles score more. Plan where things land so the right ones meet.
  5. Don't let the jar overflow: if bubbles stay above the dashed line, the game ends.

The row of bubbles under the jar shows every size, smallest to largest.
