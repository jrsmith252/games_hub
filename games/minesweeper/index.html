How to Play

Goal: Reveal all cells without hitting a mine!

Controls: Tap to reveal. Long-press a cell (or right-click) to flag a suspected mine, or use Flag Mode. Your first tap and the cells around it are always safe.

Numbers: Show how many mines are adjacent to that cell.

Progressive: Complete puzzles in order. Mine count increases!
