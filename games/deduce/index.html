How to play

  1. Each person (or pet) matches exactly one item in every category, and no two share an item.
  2. Read the clues and mark the grid: tap a square once for ✗ (can't be), twice for ✓ (it's a match), three times to clear it.
  3. Use what you've marked to work out more. If A goes with B, and B doesn't go with C, then A doesn't go with C either.
  4. Every case has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by reasoning. You never need to guess.
  5. If you fill in every match but something's wrong, you'll get a free Not quite message (it won't say where).

Stuck? Hint points out a wrong mark, or explains the next step you can work out. You get 2 hints per case: solve with none for 3 stars, one hint for 2 stars, two hints for 1 star. Undo and Clear are free.
