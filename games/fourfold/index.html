How to play

Find four hidden words at once, in 9 guesses. Every guess goes onto all four boards, and each board shows how close it was to its word.

Each key is split into four corners, one for each board, in the same positions as the boards. Here A is right on the top-left board, in the wrong place on the bottom-left, not in the top-right word, and not tried yet on the bottom-right.

  • When you find a board's word, that board is done and stops taking guesses.
  • Type into any tile: tap a tile in the current row to choose it, then type.
  • Solve all four within 9 guesses to win. You'll see what each word means at the end.
