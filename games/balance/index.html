Balance

How to play

  1. Fill every square with a leaf or a drop.
  2. Every row and every column has the same number of each: 3 and 3 on a 6 × 6 board, 4 and 4 on 8 × 8.
  3. No more than two of the same next to each other, across or down.
  4. Squares joined by = are the same. Squares joined by × are different.
  5. Tap a square for a leaf, again for a drop, again to clear it. Shaded squares are given and can't change.
  6. Every level has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by reasoning. You never need to guess. Anything breaking a rule turns red.

Stuck? Hint points out a wrong square or explains the next step. You get 2 hints per level: no hints for 3 stars, one for 2 stars, two for 1 star. Undo and Clear are free.
