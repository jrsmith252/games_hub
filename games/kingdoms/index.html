Kingdoms

How to play

  1. The board is split into coloured kingdoms. Place one crown in every row, every column and every kingdom.
  2. Crowns can't touch, not even corner to corner.
  3. Tap a square once for ✕ (no crown here), twice for a crown, three times to clear it. Drag across squares to cross out several at once.
  4. Every level has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by reasoning. You never need to guess.
  5. Crowns that break a rule turn red, so you can spot a clash straight away.

Stuck? Hint points out a wrong mark or explains the next step. You get 2 hints per level: no hints for 3 stars, one for 2 stars, two for 1 star. Undo and Clear are free.
