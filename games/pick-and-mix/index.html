How to play

  1. Swipe a sweet into its neighbour (or tap one, then the other) to swap them. Line up 3 or more the same and they're gone.
  2. Reach the level's goals (top of the screen) before the moves run out.
  3. Bigger matches make special sweets, and swapping two specials together sets off a bigger blast:
  1. Jelly: match sweets on top of it to clear it (dark jelly takes two). Crates: match next to them, or blast them, to break them. Cherries: bring them down to the bottom.
  2. Boosters (Hammer, Shuffle, and 5 extra moves if you run out) are free, but they cost stars: 3 stars only without boosters, at most 2 with one or two, and 1 with more.
