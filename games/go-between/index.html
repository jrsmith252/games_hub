How to play

SUNDAYroastPOTATOES
  1. Each answer goes between two words: it follows the first and comes before the second. Sunday roast, roast potatoes.
  2. The first letters of the answers, top to bottom, spell the mystery word.
  3. Tap a clue and type. Solve the mystery word at any time and the first letter of every answer is filled in for you.
  4. A hint reveals the next letter of the clue you're on. You get 3 hints a puzzle.

Daily: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone. No hints for 3 stars, one hint for 2, two for 1. Use all three and it's still a pass, with no stars.

Practice: as many puzzles as you like. Solve it for a pass, or give up and try a new one.
