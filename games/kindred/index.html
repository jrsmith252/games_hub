How to play

Robin Owl SwanWren2/4
  1. Every bubble holds a word. The words make groups of four that belong together, like four birds.
  2. Drag a bubble onto another to merge them (or tap one, then the other). If they belong together they join up. Four in a bubble and the group is found!
  3. Each merge you try uses a move, right or wrong. Find every group before your moves run out.
  4. As groups are found, new bubbles float in until the board is clear.
  5. Every word fits only one group on the board, though some may look as if they fit two. Think it through!

Stars go by how many spare moves you use: one or none for 3 stars, up to three for 2 stars. A hint shows two bubbles that belong together, and uses a move. Run out of moves? Try again: it's free.
