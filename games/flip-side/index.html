How to play

  1. Black goes first. Put a disc down so that one or more of the other colour are trapped in a straight line between it and one of yours (across, down or diagonal).
  2. Every trapped disc flips to your colour.
  3. The dots show where you can go. If you can't go anywhere, your turn is skipped.
  4. When neither player can move, whoever has more discs wins.

Tip: corners can never be flipped, so they're gold. Be wary of the squares right next to an empty corner.
