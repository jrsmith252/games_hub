How to play

  1. Build a full run from King down to Ace in one suit and it leaves the table. Clear all eight runs to win.
  2. Any card can go on a card one higher, whatever the suit, but only cards in a run of one suit can move together.
  3. Any card or run can go into an empty column.
  4. Stuck? Tap the deck (top right) to deal one new card onto every column. Every column needs at least one card first.
  5. Drag cards where you want them, or just tap one and it goes to the best place it can.
