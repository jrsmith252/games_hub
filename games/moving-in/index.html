Moving In

How to play

  1. Some rooms are missing from the floor plan. Drag each room from the tray into the empty space. (Or tap a room, then tap where its top-left square should go.)
  2. Doors must line up. Where two rooms meet, a door on one side needs a door on the other. A gap in a wall is a door.
  3. A door opens one way, into one of the two rooms. A gap with no swing means the door opens into the room that goes there, so that room must show the swing.
  4. Only the front door opens to the outside. No other door can lead out of the house.
  5. From level 12 the rooms may need turning: choose a room and tap Turn.
  6. Doors that don't line up show in red. Tap a placed room to send it back to the tray.

Every floor plan has exactly one way to fit. Stuck? A hint puts one room in its place.
