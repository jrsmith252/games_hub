How to play

  1. Passengers wait on the platform. Anyone whose colour matches a parked bus walks on. Gaps fill from the queue on the left. On Single file levels they board strictly in order. Tap the stop to see everyone who's waiting.
  2. Tap a vehicle to drive it out of the depot the way its arrow points. It can only leave if nothing is in its path. Cars seat 4, minibuses 6 and coaches 10.
  3. Buses wait in the bays. Passengers board the bus of their colour, and a full bus departs.
  4. Bays are limited, and some levels lock a few. Fill them with the wrong colours and you're stuck, so plan ahead.

The dots on each bus show how many seats it has. Undo and Extra bay can get you out of trouble, but each one used costs you a star.
