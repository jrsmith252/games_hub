How to play

  1. Passengers queue at the bus stop in colour groups. The next to board is on the left. Tap the queue to see everyone who's waiting.
  2. Tap a bus to drive it out of the depot. It can only leave if nothing is in the way of its arrow.
  3. Buses wait in the bays. Passengers board the bus of their colour, and a full bus departs.
  4. Bays are limited, and some levels lock a few. Fill them with the wrong colours and you're stuck, so plan ahead.

Undo and Extra bay can get you out of trouble, but each one used costs you a star.
