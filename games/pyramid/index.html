Pyramid
Shuffling a winnable deal…

How to play

  1. Clear the pyramid.
  2. Tap two uncovered cards that add up to 13 and they go: 6 + 7, 4 + 9, Ace + Queen. Aces are 1, Jacks 11, Queens 12.
  3. A King is 13 on its own: just tap it.
  4. The card on top of the pile can pair up too. Tap the deck to turn over a new pile card. You can go through the deck three times.
