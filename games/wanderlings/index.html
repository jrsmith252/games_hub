How to play

  • Wanderlings drop out of the hatch and keep walking. They turn round at walls, step up small bumps and fall off edges. A long fall, or water, is the end of them.
  • Get enough of them to the glowing door before time runs out.
  • Pick a job at the bottom, then tap a wanderling to give it that job. Each level gives you only a few.
  • Pause whenever you like: you can still give jobs while paused, so there's no rush to get the timing right.
  • Drag the level, or tap the map underneath, to look around a big level.
  • Give up ends the level (tap it twice). Every level has been checked with a real solution, and you can watch it from the level's start screen.
