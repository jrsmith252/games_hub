TriPeaks
Shuffling a winnable deal…

How to play

  1. Clear all three peaks of cards.
  2. Tap an uncovered card that is one higher or one lower than the card on the pile, in any suit. A 7 can go on a 6 or an 8. King and Ace count as next to each other.
  3. Keep going to build a chain: each card in a row scores more than the last.
  4. Stuck? Tap the deck for a new pile card. That ends the chain, and the deck only goes round once.
