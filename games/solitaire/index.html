Solitaire
Shuffling a winnable deal…

How to play

  1. Build the four piles at the top from Ace up to King, one suit each.
  2. In the seven columns, stack cards downwards in alternating colours (a red 6 on a black 7). You can move a whole run at once.
  3. Only a King (with anything on it) can go into an empty column.
  4. Tap the deck to turn cards over: one at a time in Draw 1, or three at a time in Draw 3, where only the top one of the three can be played. When the deck runs out, tap it again to turn the pile back over.
  5. Drag cards where you want them, or just tap a card and it goes to the best place it can.
