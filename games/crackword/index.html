How to play

Find the hidden word in 6 guesses. Choose 4, 5 or 6 letters at the top, and play as many words as you like.

  • Type a real word and press Enter. Each tile then shows how close you were:

  • Type into any tile: tap a tile to choose it, then type. So if you think the fourth letter is an A, put it straight there and fill in the rest later. Typing moves on to the next empty tile; backspace clears.
  • Play as many words as you like. A tally of words solved and missed is kept for each length. When a word is finished, press Enter or Next word for another.
